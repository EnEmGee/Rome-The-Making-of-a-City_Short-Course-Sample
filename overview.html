Overview

About this course

This is a sample taster of the Rome: The Making of a City short course that will be available in 2027. It gives you a taste of the features and learning experiences you will encounter in the full course.

The full short course will include text, video, interactive activities, images, readings and opportunities for reflection. Interactive experiences invite you to look more closely at the visual evidence of Rome’s history and culture with interactive artwork viewers as well as immersive reading environments. The course is fully asynchronous, allowing you to learn at your own pace, wherever and whenever suits you.

In the full 8-hour short course we will introduce you to the rich history of the city of Rome. We will discover key events, buildings, artefacts and texts that connect the city’s past to the present and the individuals who have shaped its story.

You will undertake a sample from Module 1

The Etruscan Legacy in Rome

By the end of this taster, you will be able to:

  • Identify some of the peoples and cultures, such as the Etruscans, that influenced Rome's early development.
  • Recognise the importance of the Etruscans in the development of early Rome.
  • Experience how video, imagery and interactive digital resources can be used to explore Rome's history.

Time commitment:

This taster should take you around 10 minutes to complete. People learn at different rates, so take the time you need to explore the content and activities before moving on.
